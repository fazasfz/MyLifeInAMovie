WHO AM I?

Hey, web-warriors — my name is Fatima, and if you're reading this file, it means the anomaly detector in Miles Morales's lab just pinged again. I'm a computer science student who lives half her life staring at glowing screens, so honestly? Getting zapped into a universe made of pixels and neon felt less like a shock and more like coming home.

MY PERSONALITY

I'm curious to a fault, the kind of person who takes apart a broken gadget just to see why it broke. I'm calm under pressure — deadlines, glitching multiverses, same energy — but I've also got a sarcastic streak that would fit right in with Miles's group chat. I care fiercely about the people around me, which is exactly why, when the portal opened, I didn't hesitate to jump through it.

INTERESTS, SKILLS & HOBBIES

• Building things — websites, apps, small broken robots that occasionally work.
• Problem-solving — I like puzzles that fight back.
• Art & storytelling — comic panels, glitch aesthetics, character design.
• Night walks with music way too loud in my headphones.

MY CHARACTER

In this universe, I'm "Glyph" — a dimension-hopper whose whole body flickers like a corrupted video file whenever I use my power: I can freeze a moment of glitch-static in the air and use it as a shield, a blade, or a bridge between frames. My suit looks hand-stitched from broken screens and spray paint, half comic-panel, half error-message.

WHY THE SPIDER-VERSE?

I always wanted to be part of the animated Spider-Man: Into / Across the Spider-Verse movies. Every frame of those films looks like a comic book that's alive — the glitch effects, the clashing art styles, the idea that anyone, from any universe, can wear the mask. It's the one story where being different is literally your superpower. I wanted to see what version of me would exist in a world built out of ink, color, and code.

MY ROLE

I imagine myself as a new recruit to the Spider-Society — not a legacy hero, just someone who got pulled in by accident and decided to stay. My job: patch the glitches in the multiverse before they spread, working alongside Miles and Gwen as the newest, glitchiest member of the team.
